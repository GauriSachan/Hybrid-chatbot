Machine Learning (ML) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to learn from data and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every task.
Helps identify patterns, automate tasks and improve predictions using historical data.
For example, an e-commerce business can analyze customer and sales data to understand buying behavior, while a healthcare organization can analyze patient data to identify health trends and improve patient care.
